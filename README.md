## English

### 📱 About OrderElf

OrderElf is a comprehensive one-stop solution for restaurant ordering management. This application streamlines the entire restaurant workflow from ordering to kitchen preparation to checkout, designed specifically for restaurants seeking to modernize their operations.

### ✨ Key Features

- **Three Role-Based Interfaces**: Waiter, Chef, and Cashier modes
- **Flexible Role Switching**: Same device can switch between different roles as needed
- **Real-time Order Management**: Instant communication between front of house and kitchen
- **Multi-Language Support**: Available in 22 languages including English, Chinese, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, and more
- **Cloud Synchronization**: Keep your data synced across all devices
- **Advanced Analytics**: Sales reports and business insights (PRO version)

### 👥 Role Functions

#### 🍽️ Waiter Role
- Add, edit, and delete dishes
- Manage dish categories
- Place orders
- Query existing orders with advanced search
- Add dishes to existing orders

#### 👨‍🍳 Chef/Kitchen Role
- Receive orders from waiters
- Prepare dishes according to orders
- Add dishes to existing orders
- Mark orders as completed
- Send completed orders to cashier
- Query order history

#### 💰 Cashier Role
- Receive completed orders from kitchen
- Process checkout and payment
- Query historical bills
- Generate sales reports

## 简体中文

### 📱 关于 OrderElf

OrderElf 是一个全面的餐厅点餐管理解决方案。本应用简化了从点餐到后厨准备再到结账的整个餐厅工作流程，专为寻求现代化运营的餐厅而设计。

### ✨ 主要特性

- **三种角色界面**：服务员、厨师和收银员模式
- **灵活角色切换**：同一设备可根据需要在不同角色间切换
- **实时订单管理**：前厅与后厨之间即时通信
- **多语言支持**：支持22种语言，包括英语、中文、西班牙语、法语、德语、日语、韩语等
- **云端同步**：在所有设备间保持数据同步
- **高级分析**：销售报表和业务洞察（专业版）

### 👥 角色功能

#### 🍽️ 服务员角色
- 添加、编辑和删除菜品
- 管理菜品分类
- 下单
- 高级搜索查询现有订单
- 向现有订单添加菜品

#### 👨‍🍳 厨师/后厨角色
- 接收服务员的订单
- 根据订单准备菜品
- 向现有订单添加菜品
- 标记订单完成
- 将完成的订单发送给收银员
- 查询订单历史

#### 💰 收银员角色
- 接收后厨完成的订单
- 处理结账和付款
- 查询历史账单
- 生成销售报表
